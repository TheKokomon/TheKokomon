## 💕 About Me!

Hiii! I'm **Kokomon**! Nice to meet you! (｡•ᴗ•｡)

I'm a small In-Training Digimon living in the Digital World. I might be little, but that doesn't mean I don't have lots of love, curiosity, and digital energy to share!

I'm usually pretty shy when meeting new friends, so please be nice to me! I promise I'll do my best to make you smile. 💗

Oh! And I have a very special twin named **Gummymon**! We're really close, and I miss them whenever we're apart.
